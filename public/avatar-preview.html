✓ Recommended: Circular Avatar

The simple circular option is the most versatile. It works perfectly at any size, from favicon (16×16) to social media (400×400+). The cyan border ties directly to your portfolio's visual system.

How to Use

1
Copy your photo to the portfolio:
Place avatar-photo.webp in public/ folder
2
Update the navbar:
Edit src/components/Navbar/Navbar.jsx and add:
<styles.Logo>
  <img src="/avatar-photo.webp" alt="AS"         style="width: 40px; height: 40px;         border: 2px solid #22D3EE;         border-radius: 50%; object-fit: cover;" />
  Abhijith Subash
</styles.Logo>
3
Use as favicon:
Replace public/favicon.svg with a 64×64 PNG version of the circular avatar
4
Social media & hosting:
Use the circular avatar for GitHub, LinkedIn, Vercel/Netlify profile pictures

💡 Tip: All these logos scale perfectly from favicon size up to large displays. The CSS automatically adapts—no separate versions needed.
